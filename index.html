About Me

I am an undergraduate mathematics student at the University of Sevilla. My research interests broadly lie in algebraic topology, geometry, and mapping class groups. I have participated in competitive undergraduate research programs internationally, including at the Fields Institute and the University of Copenhagen.

Research & Projects

Mapping Class Group Project (Ongoing)

Advisor / Collaborator: Nathalie Wahl (Copenhagen Centre for Geometry and Topology)

Studying the action of finite Abelian groups on orientable surfaces with boundary ($S_g^b$), analyzing symmetric mapping class groups, and connecting them with mixed braid groups and Burau covers. Developed at the University of Copenhagen.
