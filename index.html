About Me

I am a final-year mathematics student at the University of Seville (2023–2027). I am drawn to topology and algebra, and above all to the moments when one sheds light on the other: when a geometric question becomes a computation in a group, or an algebraic structure turns out to describe a space. My interests are deliberately broad, from homotopy theory and homology to groups, moduli spaces and K-theory. What I enjoy most is when a result draws on ideas from very different places, and when connections appear between areas that seemed unrelated.

I have carried out research at the University of Copenhagen under the supervision of Prof. Nathalie Wahl, and at the Fields Institute with Prof. Alistair Savage and Dr. Yaolong Shen. I am currently writing my bachelor's thesis on K-theory with Prof. Ramón J. Flores, and I am applying to master's and PhD programmes starting in 2027.

Research Experience

Mapping class groups of finite abelian branched covers of surfaces. Identified the liftable and symmetric mapping class groups with mixed braid groups, described the lifts explicitly via chain twists and multi-Dehn twists, and derived homological stability results.
