About Me

I am an undergraduate mathematics student at the University of Sevilla. My research interests include algebraic topology, geometry, and mapping class groups. I have collaborated internationally at research centers such as the Fields Institute and the Copenhagen Centre for Geometry and Topology.

Research & Projects

Mapping Class Group Project (Ongoing)

Collaborator: Nathalie Wahl (Copenhagen Centre for Geometry and Topology)

Investigating the action of finite Abelian groups on orientable surfaces with boundary, symmetric mapping class groups, and their connection to mixed braid groups and Burau covers.
